A comprehensive Learning Management System (LMS) platform for the Marek Foundation, supporting both web administration and mobile learning experiences. The platform integrates with Moodle as its LMS backend for content delivery and assessment management.

Key capabilities:
- Administrator dashboard for site, coordinator, and student management
- Student mobile application for accessing learning content and assessments
- Integration with Moodle LMS backend
- Role-based dashboards for admins, coordinators, and learners
- Content delivery and progress tracking
